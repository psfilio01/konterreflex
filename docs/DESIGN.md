# Design direction

Bright, calm, premium and minimal.

- Background: warm off white rather than stark white.
- Generous whitespace.
- Restrained accent palette.
- Large readable typography.
- One central responsive intelligence orb.
- Minimal chrome during a live exercise.
- Voice status must be obvious through motion plus accessible labels.

Avoid game show visuals, aggressive red scoring, human coach avatars and dense dashboards in the core exercise flow.
