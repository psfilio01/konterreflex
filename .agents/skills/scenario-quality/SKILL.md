---
name: konterreflex-scenario-quality
description: Use for scenario generation, evaluation prompts, content moderation or communication feedback in Konterreflex.
---

- Make scenarios realistic and varied without assigning behavior to a gender, ethnicity, sexuality or other protected trait.
- It is acceptable to reproduce a prejudiced remark as the object of a training scenario when context requires it. Never endorse it.
- Qualitative feedback beats numeric scoring.
- Evaluate posture, precision, frame, social effect, naturalness and escalation fit contextually.
- Prefer short responses a real person might actually say.
- Separate evidence based communication concepts from historical theory and label uncertainty.
